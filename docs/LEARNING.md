# Learning progress and course completion

Faculty can organize lessons into modules, assign prerequisite modules, and attach HTTPS video links. Students see locked lesson titles but not their contents or videos until every prerequisite module has lessons and those lessons are marked complete. Locked lessons are excluded from search results. Cycles and cross-course module references are rejected. Module structure and lesson membership lock once learning progress starts.

Mark lesson complete records a student acknowledgement, not a verified measure of mastery. The action requires active enrollment in a published course and is safe to repeat. The progress page reports completed lessons and assignment counts.

Faculty may explicitly enable a completion certificate policy and choose a minimum score. Issuance requires at least one lesson, all lessons completed, every assignment graded at or above the threshold, and every quiz having a released graded aggregate at or above the threshold. Draft or unreleased assessments can therefore prevent completion. No policy is enabled by default or by import/copy. The institution must approve its completion standards.

Eligible students can issue one private PDF certificate per course. The student's name, course title, term and threshold are snapshotted; later edits do not rewrite the certificate. Only the owner and course instructor can download it. It is not a digitally signed diploma, official degree, or public authenticity-verification record.

Attendance participation summarizes recorded present, late, absent and excused entries. Present and late count as participation; excused entries are excluded from the denominator. Missing records are not inferred absences. Students see only their own rows; instructors see active enrolled students.

## Course packages

Faculty can export and import version 2 FTBI JSON packages (version 1 imports remain supported) covering syllabus, modules and dependencies, lessons/video URLs, assignments, rubrics, submission policies, grading categories/weights, quizzes/questions/timing/attempt policies, certificate thresholds, course announcements, calendar events and current-instructor discussion prompts. Imports require an empty unpublished target owned by the same instructor, accept at most 2 MB, validate nested fields and references, and roll back entirely on error. Quizzes, announcements and calendar events remain unpublished; discussion prompts start closed and certificates disabled. Optional date shifts apply to assignment/quiz deadlines and calendar starts/ends; all dates require explicit timezone offsets. The package contains no enrollments, submissions, attempts, accommodations or credentials. Institution-wide notices/events, student-authored discussions/replies and external video files are not included. Video URLs are retained without downloading external media. Imports and in-app copying share the same validated transaction; existing course communication also makes a target nonempty. Authored text may itself contain personal information, so instructors must review exports before sharing. This format is not SCORM, xAPI or LTI.
