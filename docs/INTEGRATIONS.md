# External integrations
The payment portal supports an institution-controlled hosted payment link and manual receipt review. It does not collect card data, verify provider events or automatically credit the ledger. Set FTBI_PAYMENT_URL to a verified HTTPS provider URL; leave blank until configured. Finance staff must reconcile payments independently.
AI, SSO and automatic payment settlement require approved external providers and integration testing. Never send student records to a third party without institutional authorization and privacy review.
