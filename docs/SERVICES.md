# Student services
Administrators maintain final grades, attendance, ledger entries, advising notes, practicum approvals, outcomes, announcements, calendar and library resources through Django admin. Students see their own released grade and financial records and approved practicum entries. Advising and accreditation records are staff-only. GPA is a basic credit-weighted calculation using manually assigned quality points, not a configurable institutional degree audit; repeats and transfer credits are not resolved. The ledger does not process payments. Do not grant broad model access to faculty or enter real records before security review.
