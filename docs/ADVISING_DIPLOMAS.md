# Advising and diploma issuance
Registrar assigns individual advisors; advisor access is restricted to active caseload assignments and their own notes. Do not grant broad advising-note admin permissions to advisors. Diploma issuance requires approved graduation, graduated student status, and a successful current degree audit. The issued snapshot is private and immutable through the model/admin UI. PDF diplomas are not digitally signed; institutional approval, revocation procedures and external verification remain required.

The Student support workspace now adds appointment slots/bookings, support cases, success-plan snapshots, private interventions and standing alerts. See STUDENT_SUPPORT.md for visibility and workflow details.
