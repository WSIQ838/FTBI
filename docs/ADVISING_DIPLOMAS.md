# Advising and diploma issuance
Registrar assigns individual advisors; advisor access is restricted to active caseload assignments and their own notes. Do not grant broad advising-note admin permissions to advisors. Diploma issuance requires approved graduation, graduated student status, and a successful current degree audit. The issued snapshot is private and immutable through the model/admin UI. New diploma issuance finalizes an exact PDF and supports required digital signing, registry verification, revocation and replacement. See CREDENTIALS.md for these workflows and the outstanding institutional certificate and key-custody acceptance gates.

The Student support workspace now adds appointment slots/bookings, support cases, success-plan snapshots, private interventions and standing alerts. See STUDENT_SUPPORT.md for visibility and workflow details.
