# Registrar workflows

The registrar workspace supports applications, admissions conversion, registration, waitlists, official grades, degree audits and credential issuance. Public applicants verify their email before submitting applications. Follow ADMISSION_LIFECYCLE.md for decisions and conversion; TRANSCRIPTS.md and ADVISING_DIPLOMAS.md describe credential snapshots.

## Registration and withdrawal

Published courses use registration windows, capacity limits, holds and prerequisite rules. PostgreSQL locks serialize capacity changes; direct administrator enrollment edits bypass that workflow and must be restricted. Existing final grades and approved transfer credits satisfy prior-completion rules. Named prerequisite groups offer alternatives; all groups must pass. Corequisites accept an active enrollment in the same term or qualifying prior credit. Minimum grade points can be required. Students can use **Register related courses together** for up to ten distinct course codes in one term, including mutual corequisites. The complete selection is validated before any enrollments or waitlist entries change. Prior-completion prerequisites still require earned/transfer credit; selecting a course does not satisfy those rules. A full course, earlier waitlist entry, closed window, hold, approved withdrawal or other-section enrollment rejects the entire selection. The cart does not create partial enrollments or add students to waitlists; individual registration retains its waitlist behavior. Capacity locks are acquired in a consistent order. Repeating a successful selection does not duplicate enrollment notices.

Withdrawal deadlines are independent of registration. Configure a WithdrawalWindow for each eligible course. Students submit a reason from their academic record. Authorized registrars review the withdrawal queue. Approval deactivates the enrollment, records a released W with no credits or GPA points and considers waitlist promotion within the registration period. A released final grade blocks withdrawal approval. Approved withdrawals cannot be silently reactivated by student registration. Denied requests are retained. Course withdrawal windows can enable appeals for 1–90 days after denial with an institutional approval reference; zero (the default) disables appeals. Students may submit one appeal and read its decision from their record. The appeal preserves the original request, reviewer, status/time and policy/deadline snapshot. Authorized independent reviewers must record a reason to uphold the denial or grant withdrawal; the original reviewer and student cannot decide it. A grant records W, deactivates enrollment and considers normal waitlist promotion atomically, without overwriting a released grade. Duplicate submissions/reviews do not repeat the action. The original request reflects the effective approved outcome while the appeal retains the original denial. Administrative records are read-only; corrections and exceptional late/repeated appeals require an institution-approved registrar process outside this workflow. Institutional policy acceptance remains outstanding.

## Grading and standing

An active program grading scale requires an institutional approval reference. Enter unique thresholds including zero, letter labels, GPA points and passing flags. The Final grades workspace creates a draft from an entered percentage and the configured scale; a separate release action validates it before student visibility. Existing grades require the established controlled correction process; this screen does not overwrite them. Generic grade administration remains privileged and must be supervised. W and I cannot award credits or GPA points.

Standing policies are disabled by default. After institutional approval, configure the minimum cumulative GPA and approval reference. Calculate standing for a student and term using released GPA grades from terms ending on or before the selected term. Each calculation preserves a new policy/GPA snapshot. Prior results remain unchanged when policy changes. No automatic dismissal, hold or student-status change occurs. Repeat-course replacement and appeal policies still need institutional requirements and implementation.

## Student records

Students manage up to three emergency contacts, download their portal-visible records as JSON, view standing history and issue a private enrollment-verification PDF. The export expressly excludes attachments, internal notes and audit logs; it is not a complete reviewed records request. Enrollment letters preserve the active-enrollment snapshot at issuance and are not digitally signed. Student status changes made through model saves record previous/current status; existing historical transitions cannot be reconstructed, and bulk database updates bypass signals.

Regression coverage: academics.test_student_records exercises withdrawal approval/denial boundaries, no released-grade overwrite, custom grading, immutable standing history, prerequisite alternatives/corequisites and private exports/letters. Production institutional policy acceptance remains outstanding.

## Bulk student records

Import students prepares a private CSV preview for existing active user accounts. Review every row before committing; the complete batch is revalidated and saved atomically. Existing records are not overwritten. See INSTITUTION.md for limits, columns and stale-preview behavior.

## Credential registry

Use Academic credentials to finalize historical issued records, revoke an artifact with a reason and issue a replacement of a revoked snapshot. New transcript and diploma issuance finalizes automatically. Required signing failures leave issuance uncommitted. See CREDENTIALS.md for template approval, exact-byte preservation, permissions and certificate acceptance limits.
