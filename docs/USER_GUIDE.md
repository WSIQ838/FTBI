# FTBI user guide
## Students
Sign in, open a course, read lessons, submit assignments before deadlines, take published quizzes, and view instructor feedback. Use registration for open sections, records for transcript requests and student services for attendance, grades, degree progress and account balances. Use messages to contact assigned instructors. Payment references are subject to staff verification. Invoices and payment plans show charges and installment balances; verified payments have private PDF receipts.
## Faculty
Open an assigned course and select Manage course content. Create lessons, assignments and quizzes, configure questions before publishing, and grade submitted work. Current weighted grades exclude ungraded work and require administrator-configured category weights.
## Registrar and staff
Use Django admin with the appropriate institutional group permissions. Maintain student profiles, programs, enrollment, registration windows, released final grades, degree requirements and graduation decisions. Issue transcript snapshots from the transcript queue. Finance verifies receipts through Finance billing, which credits the ledger atomically. See FINANCE.md; an unverified reference does not change the ledger.
## Release status
Development preview only. Verify migrations, run all tests, review institutional grading rules, privacy, accessibility, MFA/SSO and deployment controls before real use.

## Personal academic records

From your academic record, manage emergency contacts, download portal-visible records, view academic standing and request an enrollment-verification letter. The letter records active enrollments at issuance. Course withdrawal requires an open withdrawal window and registrar review; submitting a request does not immediately withdraw you. View the resulting decision in your academic record and notifications.

## Student support

Use Student support to book an appointment with an assigned advisor, open a support case, read shared notes and success plans, and view accommodation records. Contact the registrar if no advisor is assigned. Submitted notes are retained; do not include medical evidence or payment-card details.

## Portal preferences

Choose Portal preferences to set your timezone, show or hide dashboard sections and sort your courses. The footer shows the timezone used for displayed dates and entered appointment times. Search can filter your visible courses by term, department and content type.

## Academic credentials

Academic credentials lists your finalized transcripts and diplomas. Download the PDF and share its ID and verification code only with your intended recipient. The public verification portal checks current status and can compare a supplied PDF; it does not expose your name or grades. Contact the registrar about revoked documents or corrections. A registry match and a trusted digital signature are different checks. See CREDENTIALS.md.
