# Admissions and registration lifecycle
Authorized admissions staff may record a final decision in the review queue. Applicants can view a private decision letter. Registrar staff with student-profile creation permission may activate an accepted applicant, creating a random unique student identifier. Identity verification remains a staff responsibility. This is a student record activation, not a new account credential workflow. Self-service drops are limited to the existing registration window and courses without submissions or examination attempts. Later withdrawals require registrar review. A drop or waitlist cancellation promotes the first eligible queued student while capacity exists; an ineligible head blocks the queue pending staff resolution. Direct administrative enrollment edits bypass automatic promotion. Email decisions and distinct add/drop dates remain outstanding.
