# Institutional workspaces
The workspaces page uses model-level permissions and assigned teaching sections to show faculty, registrar, admissions, finance, academic affairs, leadership and IT audit summaries. Leadership CSV exports only aggregate counts. These are operational dashboards, not accredited reporting submissions or full financial statements. Existing model permissions may allow broad access to institutional data; assign roles with least privilege and review them independently. Advisor assignments are implemented. The advisor caseload requires the assignment-view permission and shows only active assignments belonging to the signed-in advisor. Assignment-based support cases, appointments and plans are documented in [Student support](STUDENT_SUPPORT.md). Department escalation and reassignment workflows remain unfinished.
